About D-SPORTS

A Division of D COMPANY — Headquartered in Badlapur, Mumbai

India's Premier Multi-Brand Sporting Outfitter

D-SPORTS & Athletics is the sporting goods division of D COMPANY, headquartered in Badlapur East, Mumbai Metropolitan Region. Since our inception, we've been committed to equipping athletes, coaches, and fitness enthusiasts with authentic, match-grade equipment from the world's top sporting brands.

Our Distribution HQ at Plot 42, Industrial Development Zone, Badlapur East, serves as both our primary warehouse and customer experience center. With authorized partnerships with Nike, Adidas, Puma, Wilson, Yonex, SS Ton, MRF, and Spalding, we guarantee 100% genuine products with full manufacturer warranty.

From professional cricket kits used in Ranji Trophy matches to FIFA-spec match footballs, tournament-grade tennis racquets, and commercial gym equipment — every product in our catalog is curated by sport specialists who understand the demands of competitive play.

Our Services

Comprehensive solutions for athletes, teams, and sports organizations

Cricket Kit Customization

Professional-grade English willow bat knocking, grip replacement, and custom sticker application. Full batting kit sizing and fitting consultations for club and state-level players.

Team Jersey Printing

Bulk team uniform printing with sublimation and screen-print options. Corporate and school tournament kits with sponsor logos, player names, and numbers.

Racket Restringing

Professional tennis and badminton racket restringing service using premium strings. Computer-calibrated tension setting with 24-hour turnaround.

Home Gym Setup

Complete home gym design and installation service. From equipment selection and floor planning to delivery and assembly — turnkey fitness spaces for residential and commercial clients.

Corporate & Bulk Orders

Volume pricing and dedicated account management for schools, academies, corporate sports events, and tournament organizers across Maharashtra.

Equipment Repair & Maintenance

Post-purchase care including bat oiling, glove re-palming, shoe resoling, and treadmill/gym equipment servicing at our Badlapur service center.
